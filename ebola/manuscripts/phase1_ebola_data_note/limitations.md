# Limitations

- Source resources may be revised externally; stored checksums identify the exact retrieved files.
- WHO anchor pages require immutable access-date snapshots before manuscript submission.
- License and terms require final review before public redistribution or submission.
- Case definitions remain source-defined and require external epidemiological review.
- The field semantics audit is manuscript-ready for internal review, not a substitute for domain sign-off.
- The current Rt backend is a descriptive proxy, not EpiEstim.
- No figure or table currently supports causal attribution to any specific intervention.
- Hanta remains outside this manuscript scope except as a separate incubator project in the monorepo.
