# Figure plan and caption requirements

Every figure caption must state:

- data source;
- case definition;
- time resolution;
- geography level;
- whether suspected/probable cases are included;
- whether data are raw, processed, inferred, or simulated;
- that descriptive figures do not constitute causal inference.

Initial figures:

1. Outbreak timeline.
2. Cumulative cases and deaths.
3. Incident cases by report date.
4. Country or subnational faceted curves.
5. Source coverage heatmap.
6. Missingness and revision markers.
7. Rt over time after data lock.
